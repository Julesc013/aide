# AIDE Licensing Policy

This document explains AIDE's repository licensing posture. It is not legal
advice. The Apache License, Version 2.0 text in `LICENSE.md` controls if this
summary conflicts with the license.

## Default License

AIDE is licensed under:

```text
Apache License, Version 2.0
SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
```

Unless a file or directory states different terms, Apache-2.0 applies to AIDE
materials in this repository, including:

- source code
- scripts
- tests
- schemas
- protocol definitions
- examples
- templates
- prompts
- documentation
- OKF bundle templates
- generated-report templates
- CLI helpers
- adapter interfaces
- WorkUnit, WorkerRun, TestJob, EventRecord, EvidencePacket, ReferenceID, and
  related protocol definitions

## Policy Intent

AIDE uses a standard permissive open-source license to support:

- experimentation
- contribution
- forking
- adoption
- integration
- commercial viability
- ecosystem growth

The project should not protect itself through custom restrictions on use,
redistribution, public forks, commercial use, hosted use, institutional use,
model/context use, competing implementations, packaging, containers, binaries,
adapters, or derivatives.

The project protects its interests through:

- copyright notices
- license and NOTICE preservation
- Apache-2.0 patent grant and patent-litigation termination terms
- warranty and liability disclaimers
- trademark and official-identity policy
- governance, evidence, and project reputation

## Redistribution Conditions

Apache-2.0 permits use, modification, reproduction, sublicensing, distribution,
and derivative works, subject to the license terms. Key redistribution duties
include preserving license notices, preserving applicable NOTICE content, and
marking changed files when modified versions are redistributed.

This section is only a summary. Read `LICENSE.md` for the complete terms.

## Generated Outputs

AIDE may generate project-local outputs for target repositories. The short rule
is:

```text
Your repository content remains yours.
AIDE remains Apache-2.0.
Generated outputs derived from your repository belong to you, subject to copied
AIDE material and third-party material they contain.
```

The full project policy is in `GENERATED_OUTPUTS.md`.

## Contributions

AIDE uses an inbound-equals-outbound contribution model:

```text
Unless explicitly marked otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted to AIDE
are submitted under Apache-2.0.
```

This keeps contribution friction low and follows the Apache-2.0 contribution
model. AIDE may request Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off for commits,
but it does not require a Contributor License Agreement by default.

See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for contributor guidance.

## Trademarks And Official Identity

Apache-2.0 does not grant trademark rights. The AIDE name, logos, and official
project identity are handled separately in `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Truthful, non-confusing compatibility statements are allowed. Misleading
official status, endorsement claims, confusing fork names, and use of AIDE
branding as another project's primary identity require permission.

## Third-Party Material

Third-party dependencies, examples, fixtures, generated outputs, external
references, copied snippets, and target-repository content may have separate
terms. Their original terms continue to apply.

Do not remove third-party notices. Do not imply AIDE relicenses third-party
material unless the relevant rights are actually present.

## No Warranty

AIDE is provided under the warranty and liability disclaimers in Apache-2.0.
Generated outputs, docs, examples, schemas, reports, and helper commands are not
guaranteed to be correct, complete, secure, rights-cleared, production-ready, or
suitable for any particular purpose.

## Counsel Review

This policy is intended as a practical open-source posture for the repository.
Have qualified counsel review the final project posture before public release,
commercial launch, foundation transfer, dual licensing, or trademark
registration work.

  distinguishes bounded worker evidence from outer editor/tool access. A managed
  child does not establish whole-session containment or read isolation. Qualify
  the actual selected route and environment before claiming its protections.
- **Delivery:** [dependency tools](../queue/AIDE-DEPENDENCY-MCP-01/REPORT.md) and the
  [cooperative review](../queue/AIDE-COOPERATIVE-REVIEW-01/status.yaml) have scoped
  evidence. Recorded delivery is not control of an arbitrary existing desktop
  session. Preserve task ownership and do not start a competing writer.
- **Downstream adoption:** [native DiskEd adoption](../queue/AIDE-DAILY-DISKED-ADOPTION-01/status.yaml)
  retains its preparation and unresolved native-writer ownership. Fixture success
  is not target adoption, device authority or permission to overwrite target work.
- **Efficiency:** [the efficiency work](../queue/AIDE-LITE-EFFICIENCY-01/status.yaml)
  and each attempt's accounting must distinguish observed usage from unknown
  controller/review effort. Approximate character-based token estimates and
  child-only savings do not prove cost per equivalent accepted outcome.
- **Release and integration:** use the selected candidate's exact checks, review,
  effect plan and actual ref/publication receipts. The [published Lite record](../queue/AIDE-STABLE-LITE-RELEASE-EFFECT-01/evidence/published-lite-1.0.0-qualified.json)
  applies only to its recorded profile and bytes. Later reviewed phases can enable
  bounded effects; old report-only phases are not permanent global prohibitions.

## Context and evidence risks

Generated summaries and manually maintained memory can become stale. Task packets,
queue entries and evidence may describe different subjects or observation times.
A present file is not proof of acceptance; reconcile contradictions without
rewriting original observations. Generated outputs remain subordinate to canonical
records, and refreshes remain scoped work.

Context and reuse reports are conservative, heuristic evidence. Not found,
unindexed and stale are different from known absent. Structural verification and
workflow fixtures do not establish arbitrary coding correctness. Cache identities
do not by themselves authorize reuse of a stale answer. Preserve the independent
acceptance oracle when sharing fixtures or promoting useful helpers.

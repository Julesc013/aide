# Remaining Risks

- Live Dominium-owned `dominium.validation.run` execution remains unproven.
- General Dominium command dispatch remains unaccepted.
- Workbench, Service/runtime, workers, providers/models, preview/apply/rollback,
  mutation, GitHub, release, and promotion behavior remain absent.
- Non-Windows and minimum-Python portability were not separately proven.
- Older historical seam modules timed out and remain unproven.

These are accepted warnings and capability boundaries, not hidden defects.

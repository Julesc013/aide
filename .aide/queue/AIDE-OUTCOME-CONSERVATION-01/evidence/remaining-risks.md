# Limits and retained obligations

Opaque metadata is data, not authority. This is not immutable attempt history,
concurrent-writer exclusion, a multi-file transaction or universal crash recovery.
Current-run replacement remains intentional. Corrupt or ambiguous project records
require explicit reconciliation; no automatic migration or live-ledger repair.
Published Lite1.0 and pending1.1 qualification stay separate. Desktop delivery,
DiskEd native ownership and whole-outcome model efficiency remain unfinished.

# Warning Disposition

Warnings preserved as non-blocking:

- no case-folding policy;
- no full JSON Schema implementation;
- no general diff parser;
- no artifact resolver or VCS reachability check;
- no policy engine;
- no approval engine;
- no apply engine;
- no rollback execution;
- no runtime;
- no adapter execution;
- no admission or trust;
- inherited report, generated-output, OKF, and queue readability debt.

These warnings do not block the repair check because the repaired safety cases
fail closed.

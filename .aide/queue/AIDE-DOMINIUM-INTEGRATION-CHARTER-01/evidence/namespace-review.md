# Namespace Review

Namespace owners are explicit:

- `aide://` references: AIDE.
- Dominium command/service/document/refusal/diagnostic IDs: Dominium.
- Domino capability/process IDs: Domino unless Dominium assigns a product alias.
- Workbench host/workspace/action/view IDs: Workbench under Dominium law.
- bridge mapping IDs: future versioned bridge contract.
- artifact/evidence/event refs: native producer owns identity; AIDE may aggregate stable refs.

Silent remapping is forbidden.

# Authority-Order Review

The charter preserves separate AIDE and Dominium authority orders.

- AIDE queue truth remains `.aide/queue/index.yaml` plus task packets, status, and evidence.
- AIDE reports, OKF, RepoGraph, and generated outputs are not canonical authority.
- Dominium canon/glossary/AGENTS and scope contracts remain Dominium authority.
- Dominium generated mirrors and archives are not canonical.
- Cross-repo conflicts stop the integration path instead of flattening one repo into the other.

# Historical Evidence Preservation

Historical source records were preserved and may still contain:

```text
live_dominium_validation_command_readonly_v0
```

That includes predecessor task packets, predecessor evidence, and the original
independent check finding that identified the overclaim.

Active registered-validation reports now use:

```text
dominium_registered_validation_command_boundary_invocation_v0
```

Where the prior label appears in active regenerated reports, it is under
`supersedes_capability_label` or equivalent superseded-label prose.

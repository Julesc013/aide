- `provider_or_model_called: false`
- `worker_executed: false`
- `patch_transaction_applied: false`
- `preview_or_apply_performed: false`
- `source_repository_mutated: false`
- `target_repository_mutated: false`
- `branch_or_worktree_created: false`
- `github_mutation_performed: false`
- `release_or_promotion_performed: false`

Operation guard probes are safe refusal probes and are recorded separately from the main successful demo ledger.

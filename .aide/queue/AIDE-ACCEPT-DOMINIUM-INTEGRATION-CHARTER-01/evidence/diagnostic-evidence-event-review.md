# Diagnostic, Evidence, And Event Review

Accepted:

- Native evidence meaning remains producer/domain-owned.
- AIDE references and aggregates evidence.
- Workbench presents evidence.
- OKF may summarize accepted evidence.
- RepoGraph may relate evidence to facts.
- Diagnostic and refusal meanings remain typed and owner-scoped.
- Event causation and correlation remain mapped rather than collapsed.

No universal event store is accepted here.

# Workbench Authority Review

Accepted Workbench boundary:

- Workbench is not product truth.
- Workbench does not bypass registered command/service/view/action surfaces.
- Workbench does not own reusable domain behavior.
- Workbench does not hold provider credentials.
- Workbench does not silently mutate authoritative state.
- Broad Workbench implementation remains absent.

Workbench remains a presentation and interaction surface only.

# AIDE Authority Review

Accepted authority hierarchy:

- `.aide/queue/index.yaml` remains queue authority.
- AIDE protocol records remain protocol authority.
- EvidencePacket remains evidence authority.
- OKF remains explanatory projection.
- MCP contract, catalogues, and fixtures remain generated interoperability projections.

MCP URIs do not become AIDE identity authority, MCP clients do not become trusted principals, and MCP catalogues do not admit adapters or approve/apply PatchTransactions.

# Evidence Linkage Review

Case results retain evidence refs from the accepted ConformanceProfile cases and
source-chain artifacts, including:

- CapabilityManifest schema and projection reports;
- CapabilityManifest validation/check/accept reports;
- acceptance evidence for declaration-only boundaries and warning debt;
- ReferenceID and EventRecord validation reports;
- Reconciler findings for stale latest-task-packet drift;
- Track B B1 barrier status.

Evidence linkage is descriptive and deterministic. It does not repair evidence,
promote generated outputs as canonical truth, or admit the subject.

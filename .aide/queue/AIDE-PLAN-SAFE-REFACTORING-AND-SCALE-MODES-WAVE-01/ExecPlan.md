# AIDE-PLAN-SAFE-REFACTORING-AND-SCALE-MODES-WAVE-01

## Objective

Plan safe refactoring recipes and scale modes from USB/offline AIDE Lite through
workstation, LAN, enterprise, cloud, and supercomputer deployments.

## Plan

1. Define observe, explain, preview, validate, measure, transaction, evidence,
   alias, deprecation, and rollback recipe steps.
2. Define scale-mode boundaries without requiring network or service readiness.
3. Preserve all apply behavior for later reviewed tasks.
4. Validate and stop at `needs_review`.

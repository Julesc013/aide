# Project Intelligence Plan

The durable planning document is `docs/planning/project-intelligence-spine.md`.

The plan reframes Structure Intelligence as one layer of Project Intelligence.
It keeps the first executable work report-only and projection-first, then
routes later work through previewed, validated, reversible transaction surfaces.

The plan preserves these constraints:

- RepoGraph and ProjectGraph are machine-queryable operational truth candidates.
- OKF remains explanation and projection, not queue truth or protocol truth.
- Observations do not become facts without validation or review.
- Identity is stable, paths are locators, digests prove integrity, aliases
  preserve compatibility.
- Directories are APIs with explicit contracts.
- ContextPacks should eventually be ProjectGraph-driven.
- No apply-capable repair or migration is authorized by this planning task.

# Grant Model

`CapabilityGrant` narrows authority by:

- principal
- capability
- workspace
- resource refs and patterns
- mode
- effects
- network constraints
- secret constraints
- use budget
- linked policy decision
- linked admissions
- status and expiry

The grant record cannot widen policy or admission. This build defines the
contract and deterministic fixtures only; no live grant store or use-budget
consumption is implemented.

# Remaining limits and disposition

Nonblocking for this read-only advisory lookup:

- Report freshness is unverified. A source revision is attribution, not proof
  of unchanged target bytes or current dependencies; both formats disclose this.
- Ownership/reuse/test/dependency relations are recorded heuristics with incomplete
  coverage. Missing links are not negative proof; similarity does not justify
  extraction. No automatic test selection, refactoring or authority grant occurs.
- Old ledgers omit reuse data. They remain supported and display unindexed;
  explicit ordinary inventory/ledger generation can provide newer evidence.
- Input16MiB/output64KiB limits may refuse large reports. Refusal preserves the
  report and emits no partial result; manual inspection remains available.
- Controller/reviewer costs are unknown. Native job peaks are observations,
  not a whole-task efficiency or filesystem-containment claim.

Outside this child: actual desktop delivery, DiskEd ownership/adoption, broad
ProjectGraph semantics and new public release qualification remain open. They
do not block this scoped CLI result. All three job allocations are consumed.

Terminal integration is gated by exact independent acceptance and fresh refs;
unknown/changed effects stop for reconciliation, never force-push or blind retry.
The actual receipt remains local at integration-result.log, with remote Git refs
authoritative for publication of source. [Effect](integration-effect.json).

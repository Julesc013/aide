### Remaining risks

- Initial observations establish only the recorded source, artifact, environment and operations. Native Windows Server 2025, Linux and macOS results do not transfer to other operating systems or architectures.
- POSIX lifecycle guards remain product limitations. No fixture pass, collector exit or protocol declaration closes them.
- Hosted setup can fail before evaluation. Observe actual image/runtime/architecture; do not install a fallback, retry indefinitely or claim unavailable coverage.
- One local allocation and one three-job hosted run are the finite initial allowances. Changes or retries require their own bounded amendment and exact review.
- Commands operate in owned disposable fixtures under existing local/hosted supervision. This evaluator is not a hostile-writer sandbox, a provider harness or whole-session containment.
- Controller/reviewer usage remains unknown; no complete efficiency claim is made. DiskEd and other active projects are untouched.

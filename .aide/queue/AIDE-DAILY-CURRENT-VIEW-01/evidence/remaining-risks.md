# Remaining boundaries

The view observes recorded public state; it does not verify acceptance, evidence
applicability or authority. Bounded file guards are not whole-client isolation or
protection against arbitrary hostile same-user concurrent mutation. Unsupported
YAML shapes remain explicit unknowns. Read-only command qualification does not
qualify the complete daily-use workflow, a new distribution or a supported host.

Two jobs maximum. Source/effect review precedes qualification; actual results and
retirement precede final outcome review. No target, model or network effect.

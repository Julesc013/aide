identities in evidence. An interrupted run remains in its original scratch
directory; rerun only in a new `run-*` child after classifying any pending
intent. A clean source checkout and all remote refs are unaffected by
disposable consumers. Do not infer a pass from a command that timed out or a
target intentionally modified by an earlier adversarial case.

# Evidence Mapping

Dominium owns native command/process evidence meaning. AIDE may reference, aggregate, and classify that evidence by authority class. AIDE must not rewrite native Dominium evidence semantics. Workbench may present evidence. OKF may summarize accepted evidence. RepoGraph may relate evidence to entities.

Required evidence metadata:

- content address
- artifact reference
- source revision
- producer
- authority class
- freshness
- evidence chain
- what was run
- what was not run

Missing evidence is a typed blocker or warning according to the relevant owner policy; it is not silently filled by generated prose.

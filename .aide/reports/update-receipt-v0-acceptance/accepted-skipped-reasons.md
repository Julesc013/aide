# Accepted Skipped Operation Reasons

Accepted skipped-operation reasons:

- `manual_review_required`
- `preimage_mismatch`
- `postimage_mismatch`
- `unknown_ownership`
- `never_touch`
- `project_owned`
- `project_overlay`
- `local_only`
- `runtime_generated`
- `evidence_only`
- `symlink_or_reparse_uncertainty`
- `case_collision`
- `missing_rollback_requirement`
- `missing_approval`
- `validation_failed`
- `policy_refusal`
- `unsupported_operation`
- `unknown_required_feature`

Skipped reasons record refusal or non-execution facts. They do not authorize retries or apply behavior.

# Accepted Boundary

`distribution_product_status_projection_v0` is accepted as a read-only projection only.

The accepted surface summarizes:

- accepted distribution capabilities
- accepted distribution boundaries
- fixture-only boundaries
- explicit non-capabilities
- warning debt
- release/canary/package/shadow/apply readiness states
- next distribution-productization tasks

It does not create or accept a new execution/apply capability.

# Historical Failure Preservation

Historical failed checks were preserved as first-class evidence and were not
rewritten, deleted, superseded in place, or hidden.

The accepted seam is based on the full repair lineage ending in the successful
Repair 05 independent check. Earlier failures remain useful forensic evidence
for how the read-only boundary, schema constraints, fixture replay, conformance,
guard evidence, operation trace, portability, and typed refusal behavior were
hardened.

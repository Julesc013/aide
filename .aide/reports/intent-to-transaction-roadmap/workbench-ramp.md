# Workbench Ramp

Workbench should not start as a broad AI panel or hidden second runtime.

The safer ramp is:

1. Read-only AIDE workspace.
2. WorkUnit and evidence inspection.
3. AIDE Service attach and resumable event planning, only after service
   authority exists.
4. PatchTransaction preview.
5. Registered validation submission and status.
6. DevelopmentTransaction and PreviewSession contracts.
7. Structured-document preview.
8. Controlled document apply and rollback.
9. Agentic scene edit preview.
10. Scene edit apply with undo and evidence.
11. Domino graph edit.
12. Asset, build, simulation, and performance workflows.
13. Legacy IDE and offline-mailbox conformance.

Workbench and Commander should share WorkUnit, transaction, evidence, and event
state. Workbench optimizes one active domain workspace. Commander optimizes
many repos, workers, gateways, providers, budgets, approvals, and checkpoints.
